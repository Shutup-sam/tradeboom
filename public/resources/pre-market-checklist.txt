TRADE BOOM - PRE-MARKET ROUTINE & CHECKLIST
--------------------------------------------
Follow this systematic routine every single morning before the opening bell.

Phase 1: Macro & Global Sentiment (08:30 AM - 08:45 AM)
[ ] Check SGX Nifty / Gift Nifty to gauge opening gap expectations.
[ ] Review US Markets (S&P 500, Nasdaq) and European indices close.
[ ] Verify key macro indicators: Dollar Index (DXY), Crude Oil prices, Bond Yields.

Phase 2: Technical Charting & Levels (08:45 AM - 09:00 AM)
[ ] Draw Daily & Hourly support/resistance zones on Nifty & Bank Nifty.
[ ] Mark the Previous Day High (PDH) and Previous Day Low (PDL) on focus charts.
[ ] Identify 3-4 top sector themes for the day (IT, Banking, Auto, Metal, etc.).

Phase 3: Execution Checklist (09:00 AM - 09:15 AM)
[ ] Pre-plan position sizes and write down risk parameters for today.
[ ] Review open swing positions. Check stop loss levels.
[ ] Set alarm alerts on trading software near key supply/demand thresholds.

Phase 4: Trading Psychology Alignment (09:15 AM)
[ ] Focus: I will only execute structures that align with my rulebook.
[ ] Risk: I accept that every single trade is a probability distribution. Stop losses are my business insurance.
