# Trade Boom - SEO Guidelines

### 1. Document Structure
- Maintain a strict hierarchy of semantic headings: exactly one `<h1>` per page.
- Subsequent sections must follow `<h2>` and `<h3>` tags without skipping sizes.

### 2. Form Meta Attributes
- Include descriptions and unique descriptive accessibility IDs on all interactive buttons, calculators, and inputs.

### 3. Sitemap & Index
- Automatically verify pages resolve under canonical aliases on `www.tradeboom.in` and `tradeboom.in`.
